The KB and release date for each build come from Microsoft's update history ([Server 2022](https://support.microsoft.com/help/5082142), [Server 2025](https://support.microsoft.com/help/5082063)), not from the lab notes[^lab-kb].  **Nothing has been tested on a July 2026 or later cumulative update.**

[^lab-kb]: The lab notes label the enforcing update "KB5078763" and the retest build "KB5082137+".  Neither number resolves on Microsoft Support (checked November 2026), and Microsoft's Server 2022 update history lists **KB5082142 (build 20348.5020, 14 April 2026)** as the update that "changes the default `DefaultDomainSupportedEncTypes` value".  The build numbers in the notes are consistent with Microsoft's history; the KB labels are not.  Gate on the build, not on a KB number.
[^lab-apr6]: These notes are headed "April 2026 CU" but are dated 6-7 April, before the 14 April release, and record that an unset account still received RC4.  That matches a March build, not an April one.  The notes do not record the revision, so this row cannot be pinned to a KB.
[^lab-2025]: The only Server 2025 build tested is the **March** 2026 update.  Microsoft's April 2026 update for Server 2025 (KB5082063, build 26100.32690) says "Windows reads the configured policy settings as expected" for Kerberos encryption policies, which may change the filter-path result.  It has not been retested.
